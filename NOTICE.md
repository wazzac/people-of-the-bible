## Sources of the content

The content of this project is drawn from the Bible, in the New International
Version (NIV), as read on [Bible.com](https://www.bible.com). The project's
rule is that nothing is made up. Anything that does not come directly from the
biblical text is labelled where it appears, with the reason it is there:

- estimated dates, marked with an asterisk (*);
- "possibly", "disputed" or "traditional" identifications;
- details from church tradition;
- facts from the historical record.

See [README.md, "How anything beyond the text is marked"](./README.md#how-anything-beyond-the-text-is-marked).
